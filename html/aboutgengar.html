About Gengar

Hi there! My name is Gengar, and I'm a Ghost- and Poison-type Pokémon. Don't let my spooky appearance fool you—I'm playful, energetic, and always ready for an adventure. I enjoy exploring new places, learning new things, and taking on exciting challenges alongside my trainer. No matter the situation, I bring plenty of energy and enthusiasm wherever I go. It's great to meet you! I look forward to sharing many adventures with you.

  2. Lore
    • Can blend into shadows and silently follow people. If you suddenly feel colder or notice a shadow moving on its own, a Gengar might be nearby.
    • Rather than being purely evil, I am often described as a prankster. I enjoy startling people, playing tricks, and making myself known in unexpected ways.
    • Several Pokédex entries mention that Gengar absorbs heat from its surroundings. When it is near, the temperature can drop, causing people to feel a sudden chill.
